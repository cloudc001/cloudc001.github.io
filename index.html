Operations

The network is intentionally small and experimental.

Operational Notes

CloudC EdLab Network is operated on a best-effort basis for education, BGP research, and measurement. Maintenance may occur without prior notice. The network does not provide third-party transit.

Reporting Routing Issues

Please include the affected prefix, timestamp in UTC, observed AS path, traceroute or MTR output, source network, and destination address.

Public multicast routing is not enabled today. It may be revisited for lab experiments after compatible peers and operational procedures are available.
